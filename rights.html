Right to Go to School

Right to Participation

Children can express their opinions in matters that affect them.

Right to Protection from Child Labour

Children under 14 cannot be employed in any occupation.

Right Protected by UNCRC

The United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child safeguards all children’s rights.

Right to Free Education

The Right to Education (RTE) Act ensures free and compulsory education for all children.
